Privacy Policy — Soroban Mind

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Soroban Mind ("the app") is a soroban and mental-math training app developed by June Hong. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your training progress, settings, session results and streaks only on your device. There is no account, no sign-up, and no cloud sync. This data is never transmitted to the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads are configured as non-personalized. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive or store this data.

Data we collect

Other than the AdMob data described above, the developer collects no personal data: no names, no email addresses, no location, no contacts, no photos, and no training data.

Deleting your data

Soroban Mind stores data in two places, and you can delete both at any time:

  • On your device — your training progress, settings, session results and streaks. To delete them: open the app, go to Settings → Reset all progress and confirm. This permanently erases all in-app data. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything the app stored. Nothing is kept afterwards, and there is no server copy — the developer never receives this data, so there is nothing further to request.
  • Advertising data — the advertising ID and ad-interaction data handled by Google AdMob as described above. To delete or reset it: open your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads and choose "Delete advertising ID" (wording may vary by device).

If you need help with deletion, contact junehong.dominicus@gmail.com.

Children

The app is designed for adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
