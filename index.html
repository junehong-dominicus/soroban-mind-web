🧮 Soroban Mind

Train your brain the abacus way — see the beads, do the math in your head.

Coming soon to Google Play

Soroban Mind is a brain-training app built on the classic abacus disciplines of soroban (calculation on an abacus) and anzan (mental calculation). It is currently in development.

What it trains

  • Virtual soroban — a touch abacus (modern 1:4 layout) with guided lessons, from bead values to 5- and 10-complements.
  • Flash Anzan — numbers flash one at a time; you sum them mentally. Difficulty climbs from 3 one-digit numbers to 15 three-digit numbers at half-second pace.
  • Speed drills — timed addition and subtraction sets that adapt to your accuracy.
  • Daily Workout — a five-minute routine mixing all three, with streaks and progress trends.

Private by design

No account, no cloud sync — your training data never leaves your phone. See the Privacy Policy.
